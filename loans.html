Special profiles

A profile-first approach, not a checklist.

Every lender scores you differently. Instead of forcing you into one bank's box, we review your full picture and place your file where it has the strongest chance.

  • Cash salary profiles
  • Without ITR profiles
  • CIBIL deviations (550+)
  • Without plan permissions
  • Technical deviations
  • Gramakantam profiles
  • GO 111 profiles
  • Single document loans
  • Sale deed amount fully
  • Agriculture loans
  • Leased rental discounting loans
  • Notary loans

Outcomes depend on credit score, documents, property and lender policy.

Documents

Paperwork, simplified.

Send it once. We check it, fix gaps and coordinate with the lender so nothing comes back to you twice.

Income proof

Property papers

Sale deed, EC, approvals, NOC

Business proof
