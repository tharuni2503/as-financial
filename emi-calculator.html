Plan better

Three numbers that change everything.

Keep EMIs under 40%

Lenders prefer total EMIs below 40–45% of your net monthly income. We help you structure it.

Shorter tenure, lower cost

Extending tenure reduces EMI but raises total interest. We model both options for you.

Part-prepay when possible

Even one extra EMI a year can cut years off your loan. We check prepayment terms with the lender.

Review your rate yearly

When repo rates fall, your old loan may not follow. A balance transfer could reduce your EMI.
