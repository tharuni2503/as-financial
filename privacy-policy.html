Privacy policy.

How AS Financial Services and Solutions collects, uses and protects information you share with us.

1. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry or contact us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, city, loan requirement, employment details and any message you choose to share.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, understand your loan requirements, contact you by phone/SMS/WhatsApp/email, and provide guidance on suitable loan options. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Consent and sharing of information

With your consent and as needed to assist your enquiry, we may share relevant details with banks, NBFCs, or service partners involved in processing your loan request. We share only information necessary for that purpose and expect such parties to handle it appropriately.

4. Data storage & security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information submitted through this website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information on shared devices.

5. Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your contact information, or opt out of future communications, by contacting us using the details below. We retain enquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for loan assistance, follow-up, legal/compliance, or legitimate business purposes, unless a longer or shorter period is required by law or you request deletion where applicable.

6. Analytics and website usage

We use first-party analytics to understand website usage and improve our services, such as page views, button clicks, and form submission status. We do not intentionally store your name, phone number, email address, or enquiry message in analytics events. IP addresses are processed in a hashed/anonymized form for security and aggregate analytics. Your browser may also store limited local data (such as preferences or session information) needed for site functionality.
